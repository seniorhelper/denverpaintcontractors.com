Popcorn ceiling removal in Denver: test first, then scrape

Colorado has a specific rule for textured ceilings in houses, and it kicks in at a smaller area than most people expect. Here is what the rule says, what it costs, and a quick way to tell whether it applies to your rooms.

Direct answer

In a Colorado single-family home, disturbing more than 32 square feet of material that could contain asbestos, such as a popcorn ceiling, requires an inspection by a state-certified asbestos inspector first. Almost every room is over 32 sq ft; a 10 x 12 bedroom ceiling is 120. If the test finds asbestos above the trigger, a certified abatement contractor must remove it after a 10-working-day notice to the state. If it tests clean, removal is ordinary drywall work: Angi's Denver data puts it at $1 to $2 per square foot before retexturing and paint.

The order that keeps you legal

How a popcorn ceiling job should go

  1. Inspection by a Colorado-certified Asbestos Building Inspector before anyone scrapes more than 32 sq ft.
  2. If asbestos is found above the trigger: removal by a state-certified General Abatement Contractor, after a 10-working-day notification to the Colorado Department of Public Health and Environment.
  3. If it tests clean: wet the texture, scrape, repair the drywall, then retexture or skim smooth.
  4. Prime and paint the ceiling, usually a flat ceiling paint that hides the seams of the repair.

Newer homes have a shortcut: for buildings constructed after October 12, 1988, the state accepts a form signed by the architect, engineer or a certified inspector confirming no asbestos-containing materials were used, which can stand in for the inspection.

Questions

Common questions

Do I have to test a popcorn ceiling for asbestos in Colorado?

In a single-family home, yes, before disturbing more than 32 square feet. Colorado’s Regulation 8 requires inspection by a state-certified asbestos building inspector above that trigger level.

How much does popcorn ceiling removal cost in Denver?

Angi’s Denver data puts removal of a clean (asbestos-free) popcorn ceiling at $1 to $2 per square foot, before retexturing and painting. Testing, and abatement if asbestos is found, are separate costs.

Can I skip the inspection in a newer house?

For buildings constructed after October 12, 1988, Colorado accepts a state form signed by the architect, engineer or a certified inspector confirming no asbestos-containing materials were used.

What happens if the ceiling has asbestos?

Above the trigger levels, removal must be done by a state-certified General Abatement Contractor, and the state requires a 10-working-day notification before the work.
