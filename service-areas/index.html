Service area questions

What areas does Bighorn Painting serve?

Bighorn Painting is based in Arvada and serves Denver and the Front Range, including Aurora, Centennial, Highlands Ranch, Littleton, Thornton, Englewood, Lakewood, Wheat Ridge, Golden, Westminster, Broomfield, Northglenn, Louisville and Superior.

Do you charge more to travel to my city?

Travel is part of how any project is scheduled and priced, and the written estimate includes everything. Call 720-912-4676 or send photos to get a free estimate for your address.

Which areas get the fastest scheduling?

Jefferson County projects near the Arvada shop usually get the fastest scheduling, but Bighorn groups projects by area to serve the rest of the metro efficiently.

Do you work outside the Denver metro?

Bighorn has handled larger projects outside the metro, including a month-long, multi-story project in Winter Park. Call to talk through your project and location.
