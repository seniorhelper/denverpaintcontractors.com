Local knowledge

What painting a Lafayette home actually involves

Old Town's mining bones

The original grid holds century-old homes with layered coatings and original wood detail, with pre-1978 lead-safe practices applying throughout.

Public Road commercial

A busy pedestrian strip where storefront work needs business-hours scheduling and careful containment.

East-side prairie exposure

Indian Peaks and the newer eastern neighborhoods sit on open ground with more sun and wind than anything closer to the foothills.

Covenant palettes

Most of the newer communities publish approved colors, so submission and approval precede crew scheduling.

Mature trees in the core

Established tree cover near Old Town keeps north elevations damp and mildew-prone, needing chemical treatment before coating.

Mixed eras on one street

Infill construction beside century-old homes means two houses a hundred feet apart can need opposite preparation.

Questions, answered

Lafayette painting questions

Do you paint houses in Lafayette, CO?

Yes, throughout 80026 including Old Town, Indian Peaks, Silver Creek and Coal Creek Village.

Does Lafayette require color approval?

The newer east-side communities generally publish palettes and require submission. Old Town typically does not.

Is the east side harder on paint?

Yes. Open prairie exposure means more unbroken sun and wind than the tree-covered older neighborhoods closer to the foothills.

My Old Town house is from 1918. What changes?

Lead-safe work practices, plus genuine hand preparation. A century of coatings has to be scraped to a sound edge and feathered so the old line does not telegraph through.

Can you paint Public Road storefronts?

Yes, scheduled around trading hours with containment appropriate to a busy street.
