The Backyard Deck

Denver Deck Staining & Exterior Specialists

Colorado's climate is exceptionally harsh on exterior wood. Consequently, your deck, fence, and exterior trim need professional care from contractors who understand what UV radiation at altitude does to wood fiber — and how to stop it.

  • Pressure washing and thorough cleaning before any stain application
  • Sanding and wood brightening to open grain for maximum penetration
  • Premium oil-based and water-based stains for Colorado conditions
  • Cedar, pine, pressure-treated, and composite deck expertise
  • Fence staining and refinishing throughout the Denver metro
  • Deck board replacement and minor repairs before staining
  • UV-blocking formulas rated for high-altitude Colorado exposure

A properly stained deck not only looks spectacular — it also adds to your home's value and lifespan. Furthermore, proper staining prevents costly structural rot that can turn a $600 maintenance project into a $6,000 deck replacement. Prevention is always far more affordable than repair.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything Denver Homeowners Ask Us

We believe in total transparency. Therefore, we've answered the questions we hear most often — honestly and completely.

How much does it cost to paint a house in Denver? +

Interior house painting in Denver typically ranges from $1.50 to $3.50 per square foot, depending on the number of rooms, ceiling height, and prep work needed. Exterior painting generally ranges from $1.80 to $4.00 per square foot. However, every home is different. Therefore, the most accurate way to get a number is to call us at 720-912-4676 or submit our online form for a free, no-obligation quote tailored to your specific project.

Are you licensed and insured to paint in Denver? +

Yes, absolutely. We are fully licensed and insured painting contractors serving Denver, Colorado. Every project is covered by comprehensive liability insurance, and our painters are experienced professionals — not subcontractors hired off the street. Your home and investment are fully protected. Furthermore, we always carry proof of insurance and will provide it to you before any work begins.

What is your satisfaction guarantee? +

We back every project with our satisfaction guarantee. We won't consider a job finished until you walk it with us and tell us it's right, and if something needs correcting afterward, we come back and make it right. We can't promise a first pass is flawless, but we can promise we'll be there to fix it. Separately, the premium coatings we specify carry their own manufacturer warranties on the paint itself. Visit our guarantee page for the full details.

What paint brands do Denver Paint Contractors use? +

We use only premium paints from industry-leading brands: Sherwin-Williams, Benjamin Moore, Behr Premium Plus, and Valspar. We never substitute cheap discount paints to pad our margins, and we don't swap in a cheaper can after the estimate is signed. The quality of the paint directly affects how good the project looks and how long it lasts — therefore, using premium materials isn't optional for us. It's a non-negotiable standard.

Do you offer deck staining and wood refinishing? +

Yes — deck staining and refinishing is one of our specialty services. We pressure wash, clean, sand, and apply premium deck stains specifically formulated to withstand Colorado's intense UV rays, dramatic temperature swings, and freeze-thaw cycles. We work with cedar, pine, pressure-treated lumber, and composite decking. Additionally, we handle fence staining throughout the Denver metro.

Do you paint kitchen cabinets in Denver? +

Yes, and it's one of the highest-return projects a Denver homeowner can take on. We degrease, scuff-sand, prime with a bonding primer, and spray a cabinet-grade finish so the result is smooth with no brush marks. Doors and drawer fronts are typically finished in a controlled area, while boxes are finished in place with full containment so the rest of your kitchen stays clean.

How long does an exterior paint job take? +

Most exterior residential paint jobs in Denver take between 2 and 5 days, depending on the size of your home, number of stories, surface preparation required, and current weather conditions. We work efficiently without ever sacrificing workmanship. Moreover, Colorado weather can be unpredictable — therefore, we monitor forecasts closely and always account for proper drying conditions between coats.
