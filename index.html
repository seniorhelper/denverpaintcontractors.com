The call we get most

"The last painter was here two summers ago. Look at it now."

Peeling trim. Chalky siding. Bubbled bathroom ceilings. Cabinet doors chipping at the edges. In Colorado, a paint job that skipped preparation does not last, and the homeowner pays twice.

We spend a serious share of our year correcting other companies' work. That experience is exactly why our own projects are built the slow way: find the cause, remove what is failing, fix the surface, then paint.

  • We diagnose before we quote. Peeling from moisture is a different job than peeling from poor adhesion, and the fix is different too.
  • We remove what is failing. Painting over loose paint simply hides the problem until spring.
  • We correct the cause. Sprinkler spray on siding, open caulk joints, failed gutters, bare wood at end grain.
  • We put the warranty in writing. Five years on our workmanship, so the fix is not a hope.

Painting at 5,280 feet

Colorado is harder on paint than almost anywhere else

  • More ultraviolet light. The World Health Organization puts the increase at roughly 10% per 1,000 meters of elevation, and Denver sits near 1,600 meters. UV is what breaks down binders and leaves chalk on your hand.
  • A long freeze-thaw season. National Weather Service records for Denver show freezes as early as September 8 and as late as June 8. Water in an open joint expands every one of those nights.
  • Dramatic daily swings. Siding and trim move constantly, so flexible caulk and a properly bonded film matter more here.
  • Hail. Front Range storms chip trim, dent garage doors and crack brittle old paint, opening the surface to water.
  • A short exterior season. Average last spring freeze is near May 5 and average first fall freeze near October 7, which compresses good painting weather and fills up calendars.

How we work

A paint job that lasts is mostly what happens before the paint

  1. Free written estimate

    We walk the project, measure, listen to what is bothering you, and put the scope in writing. Photos work too if you want a number faster.

  2. Colors and products chosen

    Samples on your own walls or siding, HOA palettes matched, and the exact paint line and sheen named for every surface.

  3. Protection and preparation

    Covering, washing, scraping, sanding, filling, caulking and spot-priming. This is the part cheap bids leave out.

  4. Paint applied in the right conditions

    Coats go on within the manufacturer's temperature and weather window, with clean lines and daily cleanup.

  5. Walkthrough and warranty

    You walk it with us before we call it done, and the workmanship is backed for five years in writing.

Questions, answered

Denver painting questions, answered straight

How much does it cost to paint a house in Denver?

Published 2026 cost data for the Denver area puts exterior house painting at roughly $1.55 to $4.10 per square foot of paintable surface, and interior painting is commonly quoted around $1.50 to $3.50 per square foot. Height, prep, trim detail and the paint line move the number most. Our estimates are free and in writing, and 25% off labor applies to projects booked by October 31, 2026.

Are you insured?

Yes. Denver Paint Contractors is fully insured and we bring proof of coverage to every estimate. Colorado does not issue a statewide painting license, so insurance and a written scope are the two things every homeowner should ask any painter to show.

What does the 5-year workmanship warranty cover?

It covers our work: the surface preparation and the application. If our workmanship fails within five years, we come back and make it right. The paint itself also carries the manufacturer's own warranty from Sherwin-Williams, PPG or Behr. You get the terms in writing with your estimate.

Do you fix paint jobs done badly by someone else?

Often, yes. A large share of our calls are repairs of work that peeled, bubbled or chalked within a season or two. We find out why it failed, remove what is failing, correct the cause, and repaint so it holds. Send photos and we will tell you what we see.

What paint brands do you use?

Primarily Sherwin-Williams, PPG and Behr, matched to the surface and its exposure. The exact product line and sheen are written on your estimate, and nothing cheaper gets substituted after you sign.

How long does a painting project take?

Most home exteriors take about 3 to 5 working days. A few interior rooms are often 1 to 2 days, and a full interior usually runs several days. Cabinets typically take 3 to 5 days because of drying and curing time between coats.

What areas do you serve?

Denver and the Front Range, including Aurora, Littleton, Centennial, Highlands Ranch, Castle Rock, Parker, Thornton, Englewood, Broomfield, Lakewood, Arvada, Westminster, Golden and Wheat Ridge. See the service areas page for the city-by-city list.

Book your project by October 31, 2026. Call 720-208-5645 or send the estimate form on any page and mention the October offer. The discount applies to labor and does not include paint or materials.
