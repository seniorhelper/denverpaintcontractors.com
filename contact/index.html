Straight answers

Contact and estimate questions

Are Bighorn Painting estimates free?

Yes. Virtual estimates from photos and in-home estimates are both free, with no obligation to hire.

Should I get a virtual or an in-home estimate?

Interiors, cabinets, decks and simpler exteriors can often be estimated virtually from photos or a quick video walkthrough. Multi-story exteriors, older homes, homes needing repairs and commercial projects usually get an in-person visit. If you are not sure, choose Help me decide and Bighorn will recommend the best fit.

What should I include in my estimate request?

Your name and phone number are all that is required. It helps to add the project zip code, what you want painted, your timeline, a few photos, any HOA color rules, and the year your home was built.

What are Bighorn Painting's hours?

Bighorn answers Monday through Saturday, 7am to 7pm, at 720-912-4676. You can send the estimate form or chat with Ramsey on this site any time.

Where is Bighorn Painting located?

Bighorn Painting's shop is at 12191 W 64th Ave Unit 102-8, Arvada, CO 80004. The crew serves Denver, Jefferson County and communities across the Front Range.

I'm an existing customer with a warranty question. Where do I go?
