Denver house painting by the numbers

The age of the homes, the weather they sit in, what labor and paint cost, and what the market is doing, pulled from the Census Bureau, NOAA, the Bureau of Labor Statistics and state agencies. Free to cite with a link.

Direct answer

Half of Denver's homes (50.9%) were built before 1980, so lead-safe preparation is a routine question here, not an edge case. Paint on those homes sits in about 10% more UV than at sea level, through roughly 134 nights a year that freeze and then thaw by day. Meanwhile painter wages in the metro average $28.70 an hour, about 8% above the national mean, and paint prices rose 5.0% in the latest year of federal data.

Myth check: "300 days of sunshine"

The slogan is everywhere, but NOAA's long-term measurement puts Denver at 69% of possible sunshine, a share of daylight hours rather than a count of days. In 2020 the National Weather Service in Boulder recorded 115 fair days, 208 partly cloudy days and 43 cloudy days. The sun here is strong because of the altitude and dry air, not because it never clouds over.

How long paint lasts

What federal wood research says about paint life

  • A primer plus two topcoats on sound wood "should last 20 years" if it does not crack or peel, according to the USDA Forest Products Laboratory.
  • A primer plus one topcoat lasts about half as long.
  • Cedar left bare for 16 weeks before painting needed refinishing within 3 years; one week of exposure still gave 13 years.

Those are lab results for an average U.S. climate. In Denver's sun and freeze-thaw cycles, south and west walls usually wear out first.

Questions

Common questions

How old are homes in Denver?

Denver County’s median home was built in 1979, and 50.9% of its homes were built before 1980, according to the Census Bureau’s 2024 American Community Survey. Douglas County and Broomfield are much newer, with a median year built of 2004.

Does Denver really get 300 days of sunshine?

No measurement supports it. NOAA puts Denver at 69% of possible sunshine, a share of daylight hours. In 2020 the National Weather Service recorded 115 fair days, 208 partly cloudy and 43 cloudy days.

How much do painters make in Denver?

The Bureau of Labor Statistics reports a mean hourly wage of $28.70 and a median of $27.92 for painters in the Denver-Aurora-Centennial metro (May 2025), compared with a $26.64 national mean.

Do I need a permit to paint my house in Denver?

No. Denver lists interior and exterior painting as work that needs no building permit. HOA approval is separate, and pre-1978 homes fall under federal and state lead-safe rules.
