Straight answers

Commercial painting questions from Denver property owners

What types of commercial painting does Bighorn Painting handle?

Bighorn paints offices, retail spaces, warehouses and multi-unit residential properties, inside and out, across the Denver metro. It has also handled larger projects outside the metro, including a month-long, multi-story Winter Park project with deteriorated wooden decks, stucco and rough-cut siding.

Can you paint after hours so our business stays open?

Ask about after-hours and phased scheduling. Commercial work is planned around your hours, tenants and customers so disruption stays as low as possible.

Can you provide a certificate of insurance?

Yes. Bighorn is insured and can provide a certificate of insurance for your records before work begins. Let Bighorn know your property manager's or building's specific insurance requirements when you request a bid.

How do commercial painting bids work?

Commercial projects usually start with an on-site walkthrough to measure, review surfaces and access, and understand scheduling and tenant needs. You then get a written scope and bid with products, prep and schedule spelled out.

Do you work with HOAs and property managers?

Yes. HOAs and property managers often plan exterior repaints years ahead in their reserve budgets. Bighorn can walk the property, scope the work in phases if needed, and help with color approvals.

What paint do you use on commercial projects?

Bighorn primarily uses Sherwin-Williams and PPG and matches products to the surface and traffic level, including washable finishes for high-traffic interiors and exterior coatings suited to Colorado sun and freeze-thaw.
