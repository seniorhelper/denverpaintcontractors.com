Five things that change how paint colors look in Colorado

Strong, clear sunlight

Colors often read lighter and brighter outside than on a chip, so many people go a shade deeper than they first think.

Which way the wall faces

North-facing rooms get cooler light that can gray a color. South and west walls get warm, intense afternoon sun.

Fixed elements

Roof, brick, stone and landscaping don't change, so exterior colors need to work with them.

Siding material

On vinyl, darker colors should come from vinyl-safe collections to avoid heat warping.

HOA palettes

Many Denver-area communities require color approval before an exterior repaint.

Sheen

Higher sheens reflect more light and look lighter, and they also show surface flaws more.

Straight answers

Paint color questions from Denver homeowners

Why do paint colors look different on my house than on the chip?

A small chip under store lighting looks different from a whole wall in strong Colorado sun. Colors often read lighter and brighter outdoors, while north-facing rooms can pull the same color grayer or cooler. That is why Bighorn tests samples on your actual walls or siding and checks them at different times of day.

Does Bighorn Painting charge for color consultation?

No. Color consultation is included free with every Bighorn estimate.

Can I paint vinyl siding a dark color?

Dark colors absorb more heat and can cause vinyl siding to warp. Sherwin-Williams offers VinylSafe colors and PPG offers SidingSafe colors made for vinyl, and those are the collections to choose from when going darker.

What paint sheen should I use?

A common approach is flat for ceilings, matte or eggshell for living areas and bedrooms, satin for kitchens, bathrooms and kids' rooms, and semi-gloss for trim and doors. On exteriors, flat or low-sheen finishes on siding and satin or semi-gloss on trim and doors are typical.

Do I need HOA approval for exterior colors?

Many Denver-area HOAs require exterior color approval. Get your community's approved palette or submit your choices before scheduling, and Bighorn can match approved colors and help with samples.

Are the colors on this page accurate?

Screen colors are approximations. Monitors and phones display color differently, so always look at a physical sample, and ideally a large sample on your own wall, before choosing.
