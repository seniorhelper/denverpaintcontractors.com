Be honest with the room

When a room is telling you it is time

Interior paint rarely fails dramatically. It just quietly makes the whole house feel older than it is.

Scuffs that will not wash out

Hallways, stairwells and kids' rooms wear through flat paint faster than anywhere else in the house.

Nail pops and seam cracks

Colorado's dry air and seasonal movement open up drywall seams and pop fasteners.

Ceiling stains

Old water or smoke stains need a stain-blocking primer, or they bleed straight back through fresh paint.

A color you stopped loving

Dated colors date everything around them, including finishes you actually like.

Selling or just moved in

Fresh, neutral walls photograph better and make a new place feel like it is finally yours.

Yellowed or chipped trim

Trim takes the most contact in the house, and tired trim drags down brand-new wall color.

Questions, answered

Interior painting questions from Denver homeowners

How much does interior painting cost in Denver?

Interior painting in Denver is commonly quoted between $1.50 and $3.50 per square foot, and many projects are priced per room. Ceiling height, repairs, trim and doors, color changes and the paint line move the number most. Our estimates are free and in writing.

How long does it take to paint a room?

A single standard room is often a one-day job. A full interior usually runs several days depending on size, ceilings, trim and how much repair work the walls need.

Can you paint in winter?

Yes. Interior painting runs year-round because the work happens inside a heated home. Winter is a good time for rooms, ceilings, trim and cabinets while exterior crews wait for warm weather.

What sheen should I use in each room?

A common approach is flat on ceilings, matte or eggshell in living areas and bedrooms, satin in kitchens, baths and kids' rooms, and semi-gloss on trim and doors. Higher sheens clean more easily but show surface flaws more, so we recommend sheens room by room.

Will the smell bother my family or pets?

Most interior wall paints today are water-based and low in VOCs, and Colorado adopted tighter VOC limits for architectural coatings in 2020. If anyone in the home is sensitive, ask about zero-VOC options and we will plan ventilation.

There is a water stain on my ceiling. Can you just paint over it?

Fix the leak first. After that, the stain needs a stain-blocking primer or it will bleed right back through the new paint. We handle that as part of prep.

Do I need to move all my furniture?

Clearing small items, art and electronics helps and can reduce cost. We cover and move larger pieces as part of protection, and we confirm the plan at the estimate.

Do you paint kitchen cabinets too?

Yes, and it is one of the highest-impact interior projects there is. See our cabinet painting page for how the process works.
