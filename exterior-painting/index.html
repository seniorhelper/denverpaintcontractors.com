Two minutes outside

Seven signs your exterior needs paint this season

Walk your house and look at the trim first. Exterior paint almost always fails at the edges before it fails in the field.

Peeling or flaking trim

Paint lifting off fascia, sills or railings means water is already getting underneath the film.

Chalky siding

Rub a sunny wall. A powdery residue on your hand means UV has broken down the binder.

Faded south and west walls

The elevations that take afternoon sun fade first, and uneven color is the early warning.

Open caulk joints

Gaps around windows, doors and corner boards let freeze-thaw push water in from behind.

Gray or bare wood

Once wood is exposed, every storm and every cold night does more damage.

Hail chips and dings

Front Range storms chip trim and garage doors and crack brittle older paint.

What's in the scope

What is in an exterior scope

  • Protection first. Windows, landscaping, walkways, fixtures and vehicles covered before anything opens.
  • Washing. Dirt, chalk and pollen removed so the new coating can actually bond.
  • Scraping and sanding. Loose paint removed to a sound edge and transitions sanded so old lines do not telegraph.
  • Caulking. Failed caulk removed and joints resealed with exterior-rated flexible sealant.
  • Spot priming. Bare wood, stains and problem areas primed before finish coats.
  • Correct products. Sherwin-Williams, PPG or Behr exterior lines chosen for the substrate and its exposure.
  • Finish coats in the right window. Applied within the manufacturer's temperature and weather range, overnight lows included.

Questions, answered

Exterior painting questions from Denver homeowners

How much does it cost to paint a house exterior in Denver?

Published 2026 Denver cost data puts exterior house painting at roughly $1.55 to $4.10 per square foot of paintable surface. Height, prep, siding type, trim detail and the paint line decide where a specific home lands.

How long does an exterior paint job take?

Most residential exteriors take about 3 to 5 working days. Heavy prep, large or multi-story homes and weather delays can extend that, and your written estimate lays out the schedule.

When is the best time to paint a house exterior in Colorado?

Late spring through early fall is the most dependable window. The National Weather Service lists Denver's average last spring freeze near May 5 and first fall freeze near October 7, and paint labels list minimum temperatures that include overnight lows.

Can you paint vinyl siding a darker color?

Sometimes. Dark colors absorb heat and can warp vinyl. Sherwin-Williams and PPG both publish color collections formulated for vinyl, and those are the colors to choose from when going darker.

Should I paint brick or stucco?

Stucco is commonly painted and needs a breathable masonry coating. Brick is a one-way decision that is very hard to reverse, so it deserves a careful conversation about moisture and product choice first.

Do I need HOA approval?

Many Denver-area HOAs require exterior color approval before work starts. Get the approved palette or submit your colors early, and we can match approved schemes and provide samples.

My home was built before 1978. Does that matter?

Yes. Those homes may contain lead-based paint, and federal EPA rules require certified firms and lead-safe work practices when disturbing those surfaces. Tell us the year built so the estimate accounts for it.

What if my current paint is peeling badly?

That is a repair job before it is a paint job. We find the moisture or adhesion cause, remove failing material, correct the cause and then repaint so the new finish has something sound to bond to.
