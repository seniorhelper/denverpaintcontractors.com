Practical, Colorado-specific guides for homeowners and property owners: what painting costs in the Denver area, when to paint, why exterior paint fails at altitude, and how to plan interior, exterior, cabinet and deck projects.

The guides are written by Bighorn Painting, a locally owned painting contractor in Arvada serving Denver and the Front Range, and draw on sources like the National Weather Service, the World Health Organization, the EPA and published cost data.
