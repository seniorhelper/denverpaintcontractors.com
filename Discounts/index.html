Seven real ways to save

How to lower the cost of your painting project

Check the ones that fit. They drop straight into your estimate request below so Bighorn can price your job around them.

  1. Bundle projects into one visit

    Pricing the exterior trim, front door and a few rooms together means one setup, one round of masking and one cleanup instead of several.

  2. Book interior work in the off-season

    Exterior calendars fill up in warm months. Interior painting runs year-round, and winter is often the easiest time to get dates.

  3. Handle the easy prep yourself

    Clearing rooms, moving furniture, pulling art and trimming shrubs back from siding can cut crew time. Leave scraping, sanding and priming to the pros.

  4. Stain the deck before boards fail

    Keeping wood sealed is routine maintenance. Waiting until boards gray, split and rot turns it into repairs.

  5. Paint cabinets instead of replacing them

    If the boxes and doors are solid, a sprayed cabinet finish updates the kitchen without new cabinetry or demolition.

  6. Spend on the walls that take the sun

    South and west walls take the harshest sun. Putting the top-tier coating where it earns its keep can balance the budget.

  7. Fix it once

    Paying less for skipped prep usually means paying again in a few summers. Compare coats, primer, product line and prep, not just the total.

Straight answers

Painting discount and pricing questions

Do you have a painting coupon or discount right now?

Bighorn Painting doesn't run countdown-timer coupons or inflate a price so it can be marked down. Ask about any current seasonal pricing when you call 720-912-4676. The savings you can count on come from how the project is scoped and scheduled, and every estimate and color consultation is free.

What is the cheapest time of year to paint in Denver?

Exterior painting depends on warm, dry weather, so late spring through early fall is when those calendars fill up. Interior work runs year-round, and winter is often the easiest time to get interior dates. Ask about winter interior scheduling when you request your estimate.

Does bundling painting projects save money?

Often, yes. One visit means one setup, one round of protection and one cleanup instead of several. Ask Bighorn to price the projects together and separately so you can see the difference in writing.

Can I do some of the prep myself to lower the cost?

Some of it. Clearing rooms, moving furniture, taking art off the walls and trimming plants back from siding can reduce crew time. Ask during the estimate which tasks make sense for you. Surface prep like scraping, sanding, caulking and priming is best left to the crew because it decides how long the paint lasts.

Is painting kitchen cabinets cheaper than replacing them?

Painting keeps the cabinet boxes and doors you already have, so it avoids the cost of new cabinetry, demolition and installation, and the kitchen is usable again much sooner. It works best when the existing boxes and doors are solid.

Why is one painting quote so much lower than another?

Compare quotes line by line: number of coats, primer, the exact paint line and sheen, how much prep is included, and what happens if something fails later. A much lower quote is usually missing one of those, and at Denver's altitude skipped prep tends to show up within a few summers.
