Straight answers

Gallery questions

Where can I see more Bighorn Painting projects?

Customers post their own project photos with their Google reviews of Bighorn Painting, and Bighorn shares work on Instagram at bighornpaintingco. You can also ask for examples similar to your home when you request an estimate.

Can I send photos of my own house for an estimate?

Yes. Many interior, cabinet, deck and simpler exterior projects can be priced virtually from photos. Add up to four photos to the estimate form, or send them through the pop-up estimate request.

Can you match a look I saw in a photo?

Usually. Send the photo along with your estimate request, and Bighorn can help identify similar colors and sheens, then test samples on your walls or siding in your own light.
