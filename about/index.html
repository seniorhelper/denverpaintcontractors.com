How it started

A family business built on doing it right the first time

We moved to Colorado in 2019, married the year before, tired of the Texas heat and pulled here by the mountains. After years in the corporate world, a 2023 layoff turned into the push to finally chase business ownership. Bighorn Painting was born out of it.

Why painting? Because it's satisfying work. You show up to a tired room or a chalky exterior, and a few days later somebody walks in and their whole mood changes. The feel of a space carries into how a family lives in it, and that is not a small thing to be trusted with.

"Anyone can make paint look good for a month. We ask what it looks like in year three, five and seven."

That's why the prep takes as long as it takes, why the paint is Sherwin-Williams and PPG, and why the work is backed by a 5-year workmanship warranty. It's also why so much of Bighorn's work comes from referrals and repeat clients.

The name fits. Bighorn sheep are built for the hardest terrain in Colorado: thin air, brutal sun, wild temperature swings. A paint job here needs to be built the same way. Built to endure.

About the company

Questions people ask about Bighorn Painting

Is Bighorn Painting locally owned?

Yes. Bighorn Painting is a locally owned family business based at 12191 W 64th Ave in Arvada, Colorado. It is not a franchise or a call center, and customers regularly mention working directly with the owner, Asa.

How long has Bighorn Painting been in business?

Bighorn Painting was founded in 2023. In that time it has earned a 4.9-star rating across 95 Google reviews from homeowners and property owners across the Denver metro and beyond, including a month-long multi-story project in Winter Park.

Who will I actually work with?

Reviews mention the owner, Asa, being responsive and hands-on, and crew members like Edgar being meticulous and cleaning up well. You get a direct phone line, 720-912-4676, answered Monday through Saturday from 7am to 7pm.

What warranty does Bighorn Painting offer?

Bighorn backs its work with a 5-year workmanship warranty, and a job is not finished until you walk it with the crew and say it is right. Warranty terms come in writing with your estimate, and the coatings carry their own manufacturer warranties.

What paint does Bighorn Painting use?

Bighorn primarily uses Sherwin-Williams and PPG, matched to each surface and its sun exposure. The exact product and sheen are written on the estimate.

How is Denver Paint Contractors related to Bighorn Painting?

Denver Paint Contractors is the Denver website for Bighorn Painting. Estimates, crews, warranty and service all come from Bighorn Painting in Arvada, which also runs bighornpainting.com.
